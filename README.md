### about me <br>

I'm a Machine Learning research scientist/ML Lead. In my job, I work on making and deploying AI to marketing teams, and my research interests centre on covering NLP and neurocomputation for logical reasoning tasks. Previously, I was an ML researcher at Eigen Technologies (part of Sirion), and a Research Fellow in the O'Keefe Lab (Sainsbury Wellcome Centre). I completed my PhD in the Bizley Lab (UCL Ear Institute), where I TA-ed for Web Development and Software Engineering within the UCL CS Department. I'm most interested in generalisation in all its forms, including whether (arguably) LLMs can generalise or adopt human-like mechanisms of 'thinking'. This profile contains my public work and my earlier work from my time in academia. I still hold my position as an affiliate researcher at UCL.

In my PhD/post-doc, I studied: <br>
1. applying machine learning techniques to solve neuroscience problems and improve artificial neural networks (esp. w.r.t. spatial navigation and sensory perception)
2. developing software for neuroscience research, particularly for spike sorting and classification 🧠 📊

Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:carla.griffiths.16@ucl.ac.uk) if you have any questions about my work, or check out my personal site in my profile!
